No Shit Digital Agency

Description

For this assignment we had to invent our own digital agency and design a logo and a single-page website for it. To make it professional and boring we chose for the name “No Shit” digital agency. We chose a farm as the very appropriate theme and went designing. The single-page website is funny, draws the attention and breaks with all other design agency styles. One of a kind and definitely “No Shit”!
